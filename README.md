# Swiggy Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

##  Project Overview

The Swiggy Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI. The project focuses on analyzing food delivery sales performance, customer ordering behavior, revenue trends, and operational KPIs to generate actionable business insights.

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visualizations, helping stakeholders monitor business performance, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
* Track customer ordering patterns and trends.
* Identify top-performing food categories and products.
* Evaluate regional and city-wise sales performance.
* Monitor key business KPIs through interactive dashboards.
* Support strategic decision-making with data-driven insights.
